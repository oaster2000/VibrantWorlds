My primary worldbuilding project since March 2022, Nonvyrox started as a world for my Dungeons and Dragons 5e Campaign. In that time it has become a labour of love, my forever world that is constantly expanding.

Currently it is primarily being developed for TTRPG play, but there is prose also being developed and written as we speak. I hope some day to publish more content that is specific to Nonvyrox.

While this website is effective for holding some ideas and thoughts about various parts of worldbuilding and all research based aspects, its not going to hold all the content for Nonvyrox, that can be found on the Nonvyrox WorldAnvil page, which has been grown and developed since March 2022 with a myriad of articles on larger and smaller worldbuilding scale.

You can read more about Nonvyrox [here](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/nonvyrox-oaster2000)
