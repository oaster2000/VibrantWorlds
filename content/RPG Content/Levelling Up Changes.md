Levelling up should feel powerful, you are advancing your powerful heroes even further into their stories. As a result, I've made some changes to Levelling up for my players.
# General Levelling
As a general rule, everyone will level up at the same pace. We will be using Milestone levelling, so you will level as you achieve specific story beats within the world. Regardless of attendance, no one will be left behind.
# Ability Score Increase
When you reach a level in a class that would provide an Ability Score Increase (ASI), you will have a few choices:
- Add +2 to any one ability score.
- Add +1 to any two ability scores.
- Add +1 to any one ability score, and take a feat of your choice. (Feats must be run by the DM first)
# Hit Points on Level Up
You roll hit dice for level ups. Furthermore, a roll of a 1 will be rerolled. (It sucks levelling up for 1 hit point).
