This note will give people a guide to some of the Dungeons and Dragons 5e Home Rules that I've either used or are currently using.

I wanted to give a space for those who are my players, but also an area where people can see the different ideas of the home rules that I've used. Maybe they inspire you to use them in your own game, or give you ideas for new things to add in.

# Custom Systems in Use
When referencing certain systems, I like to reference back to other systems that have been created either by myself for other publications, or by others. This section will attempt to link to a place that you can get the publications that I have used.
- *Rewards and Recognition* by Owen Davies and Ed Robinson, from [Lair Magazine 17](https://thedmlair.com/collections/lair-magazine/products/coin-courage-lair-magazine-17-may-2022-issue).
- *Weapon Customization* by Owen Davies, from [Lair Magazine 23](https://thedmlair.com/collections/lair-magazine/products/warfare-wizardry-lair-magazine-23-november-2022-issue).
- *Wildest Dreams* by Eric Gronske, from [Lair Magazine 25](https://thedmlair.com/collections/lair-magazine/products/prophecy-power-lair-magazine-25-january-2023-issue).
- *Harvesting Rules* and *Crafting Rules* from [Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting](https://loottavern.com/helianas-series/?srsltid=AfmBOorxSYs-uhvaERm0bjNDnt4PXCH0JSFg9Kv-ohMP7rmAZb-YXjFO).
