# Weapon Interactions
The rules in the _player's guide_ for drawing and switching weapons will be ignored. Instead favoring to have weapons be drawn as completely free actions. However, while dropping a weapon is a free action. Picking up your weapon again will take a bonus action.
# Potions
There are two ways a player can utilise a potion on their turn.
 - **Drinking a Potion Yourself.** A bonus action to complete.
 - **Applying a Potion to Someone Else.** An action to complete.
