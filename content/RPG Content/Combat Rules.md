# How Do You Want To Do This?
While not TRULY a homebrew rule, I shall be implementing the phrase "How Do You Want To Do This?" At the end of a combat the attacker that lands that final blow will be asked to describe how they achieve the kill. This will then be narratively taken by the DM and made into the formal story that occurs.
# Tag Team
Once per session, a player may spend a point of Heroic Inspiration to initiate a Tag Team with at least one other player.
The initiating player can choose an action to perform, whether a Spell or Attack.
Any other players may use a reaction to join with another action.
All players involved in the Tag Team roll a Weapon Attack or Spell Attack Roll depending on the action chosen. Players get to pick the roll that they use and then resolve damage as usual. Critical Successes and Failures apply to these attacks.
